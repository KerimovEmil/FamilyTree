### Timeline of Aligeidar Suleimanov's Life
1. **1903–1941 (Baku & Khizi):** Born May 22, 1903 in Amirjan, Baku. After completing law school and military service, he served as a judge in Khizi, married Kursum Kerimova, had 5 children, and relocated back to Baku along with 3-year-old brother-in-law Negmatin Kerimov.
2. **1941–1945 (WWII & Berlin):** Mobilized into the Red Army in 1941 and deployed to the **Crimean Front**. Captured by German forces in 1943, he joined the Azerbaijani Legion and worked in Berlin as an archivist and contributor to the legion newspaper ***"Azərbaycan"*** alongside Dr. Məhəmməd Kəngərli, Məcid Musazadə, and Sultan Hacıoğlu.
3. **1945–1948 (Allied POW Camp in Naples & Asylum Under King Farouk of Egypt):** Captured by US forces in 1945 and interned in Naples, Italy. When the Allies prepared to forcibly repatriate 450 legionnaires to the USSR (where execution awaited), the prisoners prepared poison for collective suicide. Instead, **King Farouk of Egypt** intervened and granted all 450 asylum in Cairo. Living in Egypt from **February 12, 1946 to April 15, 1948**, Aligeidar was personally appointed by King Farouk to manage the King's vast agricultural estate outside Cairo.
4. **1948–1967 (Turkey as "Ali Haydar Aras"):** Emigrated to Turkey in 1948 under a special Turkish Grand National Assembly (TBMM) naturalization decree, adopting the surname **Aras**. He stayed at Princess Emine's *Mermer Köşk* in Istanbul, married **Nazik Belin** in 1951, and managed agricultural estates in Halkalı (*Ada Çiftliği*), Sarıyer (*Bozkurt Tavuk Çiftliği*), **Gemiç village in Gemlik (Bursa)**, and General Şükrü Pasha's **Tuzla Çiftliği**.
5. **1966–1967 (Reconnection via Maestro Niyazi & Passing):** In 1966, renowned Azerbaijani conductor **Maestro Niyazi** carried a letter, photos, and gold gifts from Aligeidar in Turkey to his family in Baku, returning with a letter titled *"Həyat yoldaşım"* (*My spouse*) from Kursum and family photographs. Having suffered his first heart attack during captivity in Italy, Aligeidar died of a second heart attack on **March 7, 1967** in Gemlik and was buried in Gemiç Village Cemetery.

### Courtship in Khizi: Family Lore & The "Bucket of Water" Story
An authentic, vivid oral tradition preserved within the family recounts the dramatic story of how Aligeidar Suleimanov and Kursum Kerimova met in Khizi:

1. **The Bucket of Water from the Second Floor:** In traditional rural Khizi, strict modesty customs meant that young unmarried women kept their faces veiled or covered in the presence of strangers and guests. Aligeidar, who was renting a room on the upper floor of Halig Kerimov's house, was eager to see Kursum's face. He devised a clever ruse: from the second floor, he arranged to pour or drop a bucket of water down near or onto Kursum. Startled and soaked, Kursum instinctively looked straight up to see where the water had come from—giving Aligeidar a clear, direct view of her face. He was instantly captivated by her beauty and resolved that she was the woman he must marry.
2. **The Conflict with Halig Kerimov & The Jail Episode:** When Aligeidar formally asked for Kursum's hand, her father Halig Kerimov (`@I6000000003253617049@`) adamantly refused to give his blessing to an outsider from Baku. As a young judge and chief legal authority posted to the Khizi court, Aligeidar was not accustomed to being denied; in an audacious exercise of judicial leverage, he ordered Halig temporarily locked up in the local jail to compel him to reconsider his refusal!
3. **The Emissary to Amirjan & The Uncle's Good Word:** Halig remained determined to protect his daughter and dispatched a trusted relative from Khizi across the province to Amirjan (Aligeidar's native village on the Absheron peninsula) to conduct a discreet background check on Aligeidar's family, character, and lineage. In an extraordinary stroke of fate, the very first local resident the relative met upon arriving in Amirjan and asked about Aligeidar happened to be **Aligeidar's own uncle**! The uncle gave an honorable, highly respected account of Aligeidar and the Suleimanov family, reassuring the relative and convincing Halig to drop his objections and joyfully consent to the marriage.

## 3. Pre-1920 Baku Governorate Archival Systems & Census Records

Prior to Sovietization on April 28, 1920 and the establishment of Soviet Civil Registry Offices (ZAGS / *ЗАГС*), civil status in Azerbaijan was maintained under imperial Russian confessional administration. Two major primary record groups held at the **National Archive of the Republic of Azerbaijan** (*Azərbaycan Respublikasının Dövlət Tarix Arxivi* — **ARDTA**, historically **ГИААР**) preserve these ancestral records:

### A. Mosque Metric Books (Метрические книги мечетей) — ARDTA Fond 290 & Fond 291
Under the 1872 imperial statute (*"Положение об управлении Закавказским мусульманским духовенством"*), parish Muslim clergy (Mollas and Akhunds) maintained official bound annual metric books in dual Russian and Azerbaijani (written in Arabic/Perso-Arabic Nastaliq and Naskh script).
* **Fond 290 (*Закавказское мусульманское духовное правление шиитского учения*):** Transcaucasian Shia Spiritual Board (1872–1919), holding consistorial copies of metric books for Shia communities across Baku Uyezd, Kuba Uyezd, Shamakhi Uyezd, Lankaran Uyezd, and Baku City.
* **Fond 291 (*Закавказское мусульманское духовное правление суннитского учения*):** Transcaucasian Sunni Spiritual Board (1872–1920).
* **Standard 4-Part Structure of Metric Books:**
  1. **Part I: Births & Namings (*О родившихся / Doğum haqqında*):** Records child's given name, exact birth and naming/circumcision dates, parents' full names and patronymics (`... oğlu / ... qızı`), social estate (`kəndli / rayat`, `bəy`, `tacir / kupec`, `meşşan`), mahalla/village, and officiating Akhund.
  2. **Part II: Marriages (*О бракосочетавшихся / Kəbin haqqında*):** Records groom's name, age, estate, residence; bride's name, age, estate, father's name and residence; authorized legal representatives (*vəkil*); wedding witnesses (*şahidlər*); and the agreed *mahr* (dower in gold tomans/chervontsy/rubles or property).
  3. **Part III: Divorces (*О расторгнувших брак / Talaq haqqında*):** Records conditions, grounds, and dates of divorce.
  4. **Part IV: Deaths (*Об умерших / Vəfat edənlər haqqında*):** Records deceased's name, age, cause of death, burial date, and cemetery.

### B. Censuses & Household Lists (Посемейные списки) — ARDTA Fond 43 & Fond 50
* **Fond 43 (*Бакинское губернское податное управление / Бакинская казенная палата*):** Baku Governorate Tax Administration and Baku Treasury Chamber (1840–1921), comprising over 15,800 archival files.
  * **1886 Family Lists (*Посемейные списки 1886 года*):** Conducted pursuant to the military conscription and tax reforms of the Russian Empire. These lists document **every single household (*двор / tüstü*)** in every village of Baku Uyezd and Kuba Uyezd:
    * Head of household (full patronymic);
    * All male family members with exact ages, physical fitness, and conscription category;
    * All female family members (wives, daughters, mothers, sisters);
    * Degree of kinship to household head;
    * Social estate (*сословие* — state peasants, beks, seyyids, merchants, petty bourgeois);
    * Landholdings, orchards, livestock, and real estate.
  * **Earlier Revisions (*Ревизские сказки / Камеральные описания*):** Revisions for Baku and Kuba provinces dating to 1831, 1849, 1863, and 1873.
  * **1897 First General Imperial Census (*Первая всеобщая перепись населения 1897 г.*):** Primary census schedules (Forms A and B) for settlements in Baku Governorate.
* **Fond 50 (*Бакинское градоначальство / Бакинская мещанская управа*):** Municipal registry of Baku city residents, merchants, and petty bourgeois (*мещане*).

* **Institution:** Central State Historical Archive of the Republic of Azerbaijan (*Azərbaycan Respublikasının Dövlət Tarix Arxivi* — ARDTA)
* **Parent Agency:** National Archival Department under the Cabinet of Ministers of the Republic of Azerbaijan (*Milli Arxiv İdarəsi*)
* **Official Inquiries Email:** `ardta@milliarxiv.gov.az` / `contact@milliarxiv.gov.az`
* **Address:** 3 Ziya Bunyadov Avenue, Baku, Azerbaijan (AZ1108)

### Recommended Request Format & Parameters:
1. **Specify Exact Administrative Geography:**
   * For **Halig Kerimov**: *Baku Governorate, Kuba Uyezd, Khizi-Barmak section, village of Khizi* (*село Хизы, Хызинский участок, Кубинский уезд, Бакинская губерния*).

   * **Fond 43 (*Бакинская казенная палата*):** Request lookup in the **1886 Family Lists (*Посемейные списки 1886 г.*)** for the respective village (Khizi, Amirjan, or Surakhani).
