Notes & Biography

Patriarch of the Gulcheev (Гулчетæ) family in Chikola (historically Volno-Magometanskoye), Irafsky District, North Ossetia. Father of Hadjiismail Gulcheev, Hagjimurad Gulchiev, Lisa Gulchieva, and Rosa Gulchieva.
Household recorded in the 1886 Family Lists of Volno-Magometanskoye (Chikola), Vladikavkaz Okrug, Terek Oblast.
