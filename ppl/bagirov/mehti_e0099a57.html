Notes & Biography

Patriarch of the Bagirov family in the historic Absheron petroleum center of Surakhani (Baku Uyezd, Baku Governorate).
Father of at least 6 children in Surakhani: Ibragim (father of Kamil Bagirov), Yousif, Bagir (named after ancestral patronymic Bagir), Sona, Hadija, and an unnamed son.
His household is recorded in the 1886 Family Lists of Surakhani village (ARDTA Fond 43, Baku Treasury Chamber), and civil status records are held in the Surakhani Mosque Metric Books under the Transcaucasian Shia Spiritual Board (ARDTA Fond 290).
