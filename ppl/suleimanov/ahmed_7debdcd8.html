Notes & Biography

Resident of the ancient Absheron settlement of Amirjan (Baku Uyezd, Baku Governorate), married to Sona and father of Aligeidar Suleimanov (Ali Haydar Aras, 1903-1967).
In the 1886 Family Lists of Amirjan village (ARDTA Fond 43, Baku Treasury Chamber), the household is recorded under Ahmed's father Suleyman.
The birth of his son Aligeidar on May 22, 1903 is registered in Part I (Births) of the 1903 Amirjan Mosque Metric Book (ARDTA Fond 290, Transcaucasian Shia Spiritual Board).
