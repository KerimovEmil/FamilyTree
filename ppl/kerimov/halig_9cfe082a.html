Notes & Biography

Respected elder and homeowner in Khizi (historically Kuba Uyezd, Baku Governorate). Father of 7 children with his wife Javahir Kerimova, including Kursum (Gulsum) and Negmatin (Necmeddin).
When young jurist Aligeidar Suleimanov was appointed to the Khizi court, he rented a room in Halig Kerimov's house, where Aligeidar fell in love with Kursum after dropping a bucket of water from the second floor to see her face.
When Halig initially refused his consent, Aligeidar briefly held Halig in the local jail. Halig sent a relative to Amirjan to investigate Aligeidar's family, and the first person the relative asked happened to be Aligeidar's uncle, who gave a glowing report that resolved the impasse.
Born c. 1880, Halig appears as a child in the 1886 Family Lists of Khizi village (ARDTA Fond 43, Baku Treasury Chamber), and civil/religious status records for his family were kept in the Khizi Mosque Metric Books under the Transcaucasian Shia Spiritual Board (ARDTA Fond 290).
