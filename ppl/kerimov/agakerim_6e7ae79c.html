Notes & Biography

Paternal ancestor of the Kerimov lineage in Baku, married to Fatima Kerimova and father of Baba Kerimov (1932-1995).
The name Agakerim combines the honorific title "Aga" with the ancestral root "Kerim", reflecting the Absheron tradition of patronymic continuity (Agakerim -> Kerimov).
Pre-1920 civil and household records are preserved in the Baku City Shia Mosque Metric Books (ARDTA Fond 290) and the Baku municipal/treasury census registers (ARDTA Fond 43 and Fond 50).
