Notes & Biography

Married Aligeidar Suleimanov in Khizi and had 5 children (Sonia, Maxura, Firangiz, Ahmad, Bahram). After Aligeidar went missing in WWII, she never remarried and raised all 5 children in Baku, reconnecting with him by letter via Maestro Niyazi in autumn 1966. Passed away in 1992.
